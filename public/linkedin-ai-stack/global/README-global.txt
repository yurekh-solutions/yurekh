## 🌍 HOW TO TARGET GLOBAL CLIENTS — The Playbook

### 1. Fix your LinkedIn profile/page for global search FIRST
- Headline: "We build AI agents & business platforms for US/UK/UAE companies — at 40% of local agency cost | Yurekh Solutions"
- Location: keep Mumbai/India, but add "Serving clients globally" in About
- Add SERVICES section on the company page (LinkedIn indexes these for buyer searches):
  AI Development, Web Development, Custom Software, E-commerce Development
- Turn on Creator Mode with topics: #AI #SoftwareEngineering #Startups

### 2. Post timing = your buyer's morning, not yours
| Audience | Post at (IST) | Their local time |
|---|---|---|
| US East Coast | 5:30–7:00 PM IST | 8–9:30 AM ET |
| US West Coast | 8:30–10:00 PM IST | 8–9:30 AM PT |
| UK / EU | 12:30–2:00 PM IST | 8–9:30 AM GMT |
| UAE / Gulf | 9:30–11:00 AM IST | 8–9:30 AM GST |
Post the SAME carousel 3x across 2 weeks at different slots — LinkedIn only shows each post to ~10% of followers.

### 3. Language rules for global copy
- USD only. Never ₹ in a global post (₹ instantly signals "not for me" to US buyers)
- Say "engineering team in India" proudly WITH the cost anchor (30–40% of US rates) — cost arbitrage is your #1 hook, hiding it wastes it
- Remove India-only refs (GST, COD, Tier-2 cities) from global variants
- "Any timezone" + "overlap with your working hours" kills the #1 offshore objection

### 4. Comment-first strategy (fastest way into US/UK feeds)
- Follow 30 US/UK founders & CTOs who post about AI costs, hiring devs, SaaS spend
- Comment a genuinely useful insight within 30 min of their post (their audience sees you)
- 2 weeks of daily comments >> 2 months of posting into the void
- Search terms to find them: "AI agency cost", "looking for dev team", "SaaS spend audit"

### 5. Google crawl for global buyers
- LinkedIn posts rank on Google for long-tail searches — the $0 stack headline targets "free AI architecture stack 2026"
- Add an English blog post version of this carousel on yurekh.com (/blog/zero-dollar-ai-stack-2026) with the same content + JSON-LD Article schema → double crawl surface
- Alt-text every slide (below) — indexed by Google Images

### 6. DM funnel for international leads
- Trigger word: "AI STACK" → reply with Calendly/Cal.com link (free) + 3 relevant case bullets
- First call: 30 min, architecture only, NO pricing pitch — global buyers buy trust first
- Follow up with a 1-page PDF scope within 24h (they compare you against $150/hr locals — speed wins)
