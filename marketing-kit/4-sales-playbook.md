## PART 2 — REFERRAL ASK (use after EVERY delivered project)

### The message (send 3–7 days after delivery, while satisfaction is peak)
```
Hi [Name], really glad the [project] is live and performing 🎉

One small ask: most of our best clients come through introductions.

If you know ONE business owner who needs what we just built for you,
an intro earns you 10% off your next project with us — and they get
priority onboarding.

No pressure — but if someone comes to mind, just connect us on
WhatsApp and I'll take it from there.
```

### Rules that make referrals actually work
1. Ask EVERY client, EVERY project — the ask is the system
2. Ask for ONE intro, not "anyone you know" (specific = actionable)
3. Reward both sides (10% off for them, priority for the referred)
4. When an intro lands, update the referrer: "Your intro became a
   client — your 10% is locked in." That triggers the NEXT referral.

2. Agree the handshake: warm WhatsApp intro, not just a name-drop
3. Agree turnaround promise (both sides reply to referred leads < 24h)
4. Swap 2–3 sentence "how to introduce us" blurbs
5. WhatsApp group with both of you for frictionless intros
